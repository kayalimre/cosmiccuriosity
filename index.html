Cosmic Curiosity

Mind-blowing "What if?" science questions, answered with what real science says would actually happen.

Cosmic Curiosity is an English-language science channel on YouTube. Each video explores one fascinating question about space, astronomy, physics, planet Earth, the human body, time, extreme environments or the future of humanity, and explains the science behind it in simple, clear language.

About the Cosmic Curiosity app

The Cosmic Curiosity app is a private publishing tool used only by the channel owner to upload and organise the owner's own videos through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account or data.
